    EVERYDAY GUIDANCE

    Practical tips for all BMI categories

    • Drink plenty of water and stay hydrated.
    • Combine diet with regular physical activity tailored to your fitness level.
    • Track progress and adjust calorie intake or macronutrient balance as needed.
    • Use tools like BMI calculators and meal planners to personalize your diet plan.

    Sample meal planning

    • Breakfast: Oatmeal with nuts and fruit or eggs with avocado toast.
    • Lunch: Grilled chicken or tofu salad with olive oil dressing.
    • Dinner: Baked fish or lean meat with vegetables and quinoa or brown rice.
    • Snacks: Yogurt, fruit, nuts, or hummus with vegetables.

    By aligning your diet with your BMI category, you can support healthy weight gain, maintenance, or loss, improve nutrient intake, and reduce the risk of weight-related health issues.
